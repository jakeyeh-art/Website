About

For the past six years, I woke up questioning the reason for my existence in this world. There has got to be more to life than sitting in front of a computer for eight hours a day, I thought.

Finally, I left a corporate career to pursue my passion for representational art.

Art is an open ended question with no definitive answer, and that is exactly what keeps my curiosity alive. It is how I connect to the world and try to become a better person, one lesson at a time.

It is a lifelong journey of patience, discipline, and discovery.

Jake Yeh
