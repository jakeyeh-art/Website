Soon after graduating with a business degree and getting a job in corporate America, I began to ask myself the greater questions. Roughly seven years ago, "There's got to be more to life than sitting in front of a computer for eight hours a day," I thought. I started to pick up a pencil and a pad. I had no idea what I was doing. I knew I was doing this for myself.

For me, art is an open-ended question with no definitive answer, and that's exactly what keeps my curiosity alive. It's how I connect to the world and try to become a better person, one sketch at a time. It's a lifelong journey of patience, discipline, and self-discovery.
