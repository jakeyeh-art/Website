"For the past six years, I woke up questioning the reason for my existence in this world. There's got to be more to life than sitting in front of a computer for eight hours a day, I thought. Finally, I left a corporate career to pursue my passion for representational art. Art is an open ended question with no definitive answer, and that's exactly what keeps my curiosity alive. It's how I connect to the world and try to become a better person, one lesson at a time. It's a lifelong journey of patience, discipline, and discovery."
