"I left a desk job to paint from life because I wanted to slow down enough to actually see something — a wrist, a fold of cloth, the particular grey of a New Jersey afternoon. Painting outdoors, direct from observation, is how I stay honest: the light won't wait for me to get comfortable, so neither can I."
