Be My Patrons

Every contribution helps build toward what's next, covering studio costs, materials, and the education that will define Jake's next chapter as an artist.

How it works

  1. Send Jake up to 5 images you'd like to see painted.
  2. Jake will make his selection based on his preferences.
  3. Selected works will be posted here on the website.*
  4. Like it? Take it home with a suggested donation of $100 (as of 2026).

Founding Patron perk

Anyone who donates more than $1000 total by the end of 2028 becomes a Founding Patron. You'll receive 50% off one painting**, redeemable anytime after 2028, as a thank you for supporting Jake's artistic journey early on.

Send an image Venmo @jakeyeh

The fine print

  • Suggested subjects include portraiture, figurative work, and landscapes. Please submit the highest quality images you can, ideally with clear, well-defined light and shadow.
  • Paid patrons receive priority consideration when selecting which images to paint.
  • The artist retains full creative control over the final work.
  • All pieces will not exceed 10 inches in either length or width.
  • For donations exceeding $100, Jake covers domestic (US) shipping.
  • Patronage is never an obligation. These works are offered, not pushed. Purchase only if the piece genuinely resonates with you.

*You will have the exclusive opportunity to take it home within 7 days before the sale opens to other patrons.

** This discount applies to independently sold work and may not apply to pieces represented by a gallery or subject to other exclusive representation agreements.
